Stress Testing Prospective Multimodal Dataset Utility Prediction

Can we screen audio–text training sources before paying the cost of training on every candidate? MuSeSelect finds a sharp resolution limit: cheap geometry identifies the right source corpus, but cannot reliably order nearby buckets inside it. A staged shortlist preserves most of the useful gain while avoiding most candidate runs.

Yurong Cheng · Wei Wu — Beijing Institute of Technology

01 — Problem

Dataset quality is not a property of the dataset alone.

Utility depends jointly on the candidate source, target task, downstream learner, training recipe, and evaluation metric. A clean dataset can still be harmful for a particular target. A score computed from the eventual transfer outcome arrives too late to save compute.

We therefore distinguish source routing, within-source bucket ranking, and selective abstention. Each decision is evaluated only after its prediction has been sealed.

02 — Protocol

A visible boundary between evidence and evaluation.

The selector sees candidate data, target-training anchors, model metadata, and low-cost signatures. Realized utility, validation labels, and test data stay sealed.

Route

Choose a source

Compare corpus-level compatibility using common frozen representations.

Rank

Order its buckets

Ask whether the same evidence resolves smaller, more similar candidate blocks.

Decide

Select or abstain

Calibrate rejection on independent targets without opening held-out utilities.

03 — Findings

A useful screening signal has a measurable resolution limit.

Centroid distance routes sources reliably for one fixed logistic recipe because between-source separation dominates bucket variation. The same signal becomes uninformative inside a source, while modality imbalance and sample-wise gating explain further route failures.

Actionable compromise

Top 2

Route first, probe second

Frozen before a new repartition family was opened, the top-two rule attains +0.01084 mean safe utility, with 6.7% pre-fallback harm and 87.5% fewer full candidate runs. Its descriptive target-and-salt interval is [+0.00293, +0.01957].
